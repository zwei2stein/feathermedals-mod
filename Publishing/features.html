Adorning Ceremony

Requires a Leader, Moral Guide, Lead Warrior, or Lead Sage. Tribe members can spectate for +quality.

Attendance: Up to 40% of quality Venue: Up to 40% of quality Tale of Honor: 20% of quality

Awardee mood: +4 (Poor) to +20 (Legendary) for 15 days | Witnesses: +3 mood for 5 days

Dynamic Trait System

Trait Removal (10% chance unless noted):

Delicate → Tough (Wounded in Combat, Wounded Many Times)
Nervous/Volatile → Steady/Iron-Willed (Slit Throat + Scalp, 5% for Iron-Willed)
Wimp → Brawler/Nimble (Touched Enemy, Struck 4 Blows)
Slow → Fast/Very Fast (Touched Enemy 5 Times)
Slow Learner → Fast Learner/Great Memory (Cunning and Wisdom)

All feathers provide: Beauty +0.1, No nudity penalty
