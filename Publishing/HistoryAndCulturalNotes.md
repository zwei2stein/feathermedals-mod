# Cultural and heritage notes

## Feathers

This mod draws deeply from actual history, but is a fictional take on it.

Some feathers only make sense in the context of the Rim or the mod they are extending.

Since there are no eagles on the Rim, these are not eagle feathers.

## Scalping

Colonists introduced and paid scalp bounties, offering rewards for the scalps of enemy combatants.

Various Indigenous nations then adopted and practiced scalping.

Warfare and bounty systems helped spread and intensify the practice.

On the Rim, this is mirrored by only outlander traders and Imperial tribute collectors accepting scalps in trade.
