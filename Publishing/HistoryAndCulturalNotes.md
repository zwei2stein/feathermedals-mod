# Cultural and heritage notes

## Feathers

This mod draws deeply from actual history, but is a fictional take on it.

Some feathers only make sense in the context of the Rim or the mod they are extending.

Feather trophies IRL are usually eagle feathers and have special meaning. Since there are no eagles on the Rim, this mod's feather trophies are not eagle feathers.

## Scalping

Scalping was practiced by some Indigenous peoples before European contact.

Colonists later introduced and paid scalp bounties, offering rewards for the scalps of enemy combatants.

Warfare and bounty systems helped spread and intensify the practice.

On the Rim, this is mirrored by only outlander traders and Imperial tribute collectors accepting scalps in trade.

Scalp pole building and scalp belts are claimed to have existed, but do not have reliable sources. They are included for gameplay reasons, not as a claim that these things existed.
