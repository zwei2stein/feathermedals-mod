# Cultural and heritage notes

## Feathers

This mod draws deeply from actual history, but is a fictional take on it.

Some feathers only make sense in context of rim or mod they are extending.

Since there are no eagles in Rim, these are not eagle feathers.

## Scalping

Colonists introduced and paid scalp bounties, offering rewards for the scalps of enemy combatants.

Various Indigenous nations then adopted and practiced scalping.

Warfare and bounty systems helped spread and intensify the practice.

In rim, this is mirrored with only Outlander traders and Imperial Tribute collectors accepting scalps in trade.

[More reading 4](https://online.ucpress.edu/ch/article/100/2/4/196102/The-California-Indian-Scalp-Bounty-MythEvidence-of)
