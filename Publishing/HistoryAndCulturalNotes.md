# Cultural and heritage notes

## Feathers

This mod draws deeply from actual history, but is a fictional take on it.

Some feathers only make sense in the context of the Rim or the mod they are extending.

Feather trophies IRL are usually eagle feathers and have special meaning. Since there are no eagles on the Rim, this mod's feather trophies are not eagle feathers.

## Scalping

Scalping was practiced by some Indigenous peoples before European contact.

Colonists later introduced and paid scalp bounties, offering rewards for the scalps of enemy combatants.

Warfare and bounty systems helped spread and intensify the practice.

On the Rim, this is mirrored by the fact that only outlander traders and Imperial tribute collectors accept scalps in trade.

Scalp poles and scalp belts are claimed to have existed, but there are no reliable sources for them. They are included for gameplay reasons, not as a claim that they existed.

## Tongue Cutting

There is no historical evidence of human tongues worn as a necklace, but taking tongues as trophies is historical fact:

* The Apa Tani ropi ceremony of taking an enemy's tongue and burying it in a bamboo receptacle.

* Konyak Naga headhunting ceremonies, in which the tongue was then ritually disposed of.

Both practicing cultures are located in northeast India.
