
[h1]Tribal Trophies[/h1]

"Medals" for tribal playthrough. Feathers worn as headdresses, reminders of bravery and skill.

[h2]Features[/h2] 

[list]
    [*]Wearable feather trophies immortalizing pawns' deeds and providing minor bonuses.
    [*]Ceremony to award feathers to deserving warriors
[/list]

[h3]Crafting[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Crafting feathers is unlocked by researching "Warrior Honour".
    [*]Crafted at the Crafting Spot, Hand Tailoring Bench, or Electric Tailoring Bench.
    [*]Requires Pristine Feather, obtained by butchering birds or refining bird leather.
[/list]

[h2]Disclaimers[/h2]

[h3]Fork[/h3]

This mod is a heavily modified fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692393053]Rocket's Medals[/url]. Both mods do similar things and can be used alongside each other. This mod was reconceptualized for a neolithic/tribal setting and has slightly different features.

[h3]Cultural heritage[/h3]

This mod draws deeply from actual history (1), (2), but is a fictional take on it. Since there are no eagles in RimWorld, these are not eagle feathers.
