[h1]Tribal Trophies[/h1]

Tribal trophies (feather headdresses) that can be awarded to pawns. And scalps that can be taken.

[h2]Features[/h2] 

[h3]Feather Trophies[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Wearable feather trophies immortalizing pawns' deeds and providing minor bonuses.
    [*]Ceremony to award feathers to deserving warriors
    [*]Ideoligion precept about Honoring Warriors.
[/list]

[h4]Crafting[/h4]

[list]
    [*]Crafting feathers is unlocked by researching "Warrior Honour".
    [*]Crafted at the Crafting Spot, Hand Tailoring Bench, or Electric Tailoring Bench.
    [*]Requires Pristine Feather, obtained by butchering feathered beasts or refining their leather.
[/list]

[h3]Scalp Trophies[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Taking enemy scalps.
    [*]Outlander factions will buy scalps as well as Imperial tribute takers.
    [*]Ideoligion precept about taking scalps.
[/list]

[h3]Buildings[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Feathered Dream catcher, a wall-mounted building that grants better sleep. Available for Totemic, Rustic and Animalist styles.
    [*]Scalp Pole, a storage and display building for scalps.
[/list]

[h2]Disclaimers[/h2]

[h3]Fork[/h3]

This mod is a heavily modified fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692393053]Rocket's Medals[/url]. No original graphics is left and original code is more of and inspiration at this point. 

This mod was reconceptualized for a neolithic/tribal setting and has slightly different features.

Both mods do similar things and can be used alongside each other. This mod, however fixed lots of bugs and at same time took another route by dropping/adding features, so do not expect things to work exactly same way.
