[h2]Disclaimers[/h2]

This mod is heavily modified fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692393053]Rocket's Medals[/url], both mods do similar things and can be used along each other. This mod got rid of most of AI code crud, all of AI art, UI bloat and was reconceptualized for tribal setting.

This mod draws deeply from actual history, but is fictional take on it. Since there are no eagles in RimWorld, those are not eagle feathers.
