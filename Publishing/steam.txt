[h1]Tribal Trophies[/h1]

Tribal trophies (feather headdresses) that pawns can be adorned with, and scalps that can be taken.

[h2]Features[/h2] 

[h3]Feather trophies[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Wearable feather trophies immortalizing pawns' deeds and providing minor bonuses.
    [*]Adorning ceremony to honor deserving warriors with trophies.
    [*]Ideoligion precept about Honoring Warriors.
    [*]Visitors and raiders can come wearing trophies of their own.
[/list]

[img=https://i.imgur.com/70Qyduq.png]

[img=https://i.imgur.com/vCsKiHM.png]

[h3]Crafting[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Crafting trophies is unlocked by researching "Warrior Honor".
    [*]Crafted at the Crafting Spot, Hand Tailoring Bench or Electric Tailoring Bench.
    [*]Requires pristine feathers, obtained by butchering feathered beasts or refining their leather.
[/list]

[img=https://i.imgur.com/hekTsVn.png]

[h3]Scalp trophies[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Taking enemy scalps.
    [*]Outlander factions and Imperial tribute collectors will buy scalps. [spoiler]Giving scalps as a gift might not be appreciated.[/spoiler]
    [*]Ideoligion precept about taking scalps.
    [*]Scalped pawns get a permanent, painful scar.
    [*]Visitors and raiders can come bearing old scalping scars.
    [*][spoiler]New violent scalping mental breaks.[/spoiler] Keep your colonists happy.
    [*][spoiler]New raid type, "Scalp Hunt".[/spoiler] Keep your neighbors happy too.
    [*]Scalp belt item, wearable belt made of enemy scalps. Skull belt item, wearable belt made of skulls.
    [*]Visitors and raiders can come wearing scalp and skull belts of their own. Settlements of factions whose ideoligion allows scalping can have scalp poles.
    [*][spoiler]Factions can ask you to hunt their enemies for scalps.[/spoiler] The target faction might not be fond of the idea.
[/list]

[img=https://i.imgur.com/nBVOX9E.png]

[h3]Buildings[/h3]

[list]
    [*]Feathered Dream Catcher, a wall-mounted building that grants better sleep. Available for Totemic, Rustic and Animalist styles.
    [*]Scalp Pole, a storage and display building for scalps.
[/list]

[h2]Disclaimers[/h2]

[h3]Fork[/h3]

This mod is a heavily modified fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692393053]Rocket's Medals[/url]. No original graphics are left, and the original code is more of an inspiration at this point. 

This mod was reconceptualized for a neolithic/tribal setting and has slightly different features.

Both mods do similar things and can be used alongside each other. This mod, however, fixed lots of bugs and at the same time took another route by dropping/adding features, so do not expect things to work exactly the same way.
