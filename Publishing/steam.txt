
[h1]Tribal Medals[/h1]

Medals for tribal playthrough. Craftable feathers worn on head, a headdress, as reminder of bravery and skill.

[h2]Features[/h2] 

[list]
    [*]Research "Warrior honour" to unlock crafting.
    [*]Craft feathers comemorating deeds - gold or silver, dye and bird leather
    [*]Award feathers to deserving warriors
[/list]
