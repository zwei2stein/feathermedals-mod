
[h1]Tribal Trophies[/h1]

"Medals" for tribal playthrough. Feathers worn as headdresses, reminders of bravery and skill.

[h2]Disclaimers[/h2]

This mod is a heavily modified fork of [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3692393053]Rocket's Medals[/url]. Both mods do similar things and can be used alongside each other. This mod removes most of the AI code crud, all the AI art, and UI bloat, and was reconceptualized for a tribal setting.

This mod draws deeply from actual history, but is a fictional take on it. Since there are no eagles in RimWorld, these are not eagle feathers.
